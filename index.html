Limitations
The secret key and authenticated checkpoint exist only in this page session. Reset, a new drive or reload discards the verifier for the previous capture. Exports preserve evidence for inspection. Verification assumes the recorder was trustworthy at capture.

    
This simulation illustrates cybersecurity risk-management ideas related to ISO/SAE 21434. It makes no claim of compliance.
